Information by topic

01Getting here

The whole festival takes place in one venue – the Raanana Pais Music Center. The four stages are close together, so every show is a few steps away.

Navigate with Waze Open in Google Maps →

The venue and its stages →

02Tickets and passes

Tickets are sold on the Raanana Municipality ticket site. You can buy a ticket for one show, a day pass for all of that day’s shows, a full festival pass or a themed pass.

Shows on the Festival Plaza are free.

The 2026 programme and tickets will be announced soon.

All ticket options →

03Families and children

The Festival Plaza is the open heart of the festival: shows for the whole family, free of charge.

In 2025 the Plaza hosted, among others, the Raanana Big Band, the Isradixie Band and “Yaldojazz” on Saturday morning.

See the line-up →

04Accessibility

We want everyone to enjoy the festival. Details of the accessibility arrangements and how to contact us about them are in the accessibility statement.

Accessibility statement →

05Saturday at the festival

The festival runs until Saturday night. In 2025, Saturday had a children’s show at 11:00, a musical conversation at 12:00, and the evening shows began at 17:30.

Saturday times for 2026 will be published with the programme.

06Workshops for young musicians

Alongside the shows there are jazz workshops for musicians aged 14–25: ensemble playing, instrument workshops and masterclasses with international artists – ending with a performance on the festival stage.

01Tickets

Where do I buy tickets?
On the Raanana Municipality ticket site. All the links are on the tickets page.
Is there a day pass?
Yes. A day pass covers all of that day's shows at a better price.
Is there a full festival pass?
Yes, and themed passes too.
Are all shows ticketed?
No. Shows on the Festival Plaza are free.

02General

When is the festival?
10–12 December 2026, Thursday to Saturday.
Where does it take place?
At the Raanana Pais Music Center, on four stages. About the venue.
When will the 2026 programme be out?
Soon. The first news will be on World Jazz Music on Instagram.
Are there shows for children?
Yes. The Festival Plaza has shows for the whole family, free of charge.
Is the festival accessible?
All the details are in the accessibility statement.
