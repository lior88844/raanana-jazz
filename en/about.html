World Jazz Music

The Raanana Jazz Festival brings days of jazz and world music to the city – artists from Israel and around the world, original productions created for the festival, workshops for young musicians and open shows for the whole family.

Who we are

The festival is produced by the Raanana Pais Music Center and the Raanana Municipality, with the support of the Ministry of Regional Cooperation. Artistic direction is by pianist and composer Amit Friedman.

Alongside the concerts, the festival is also a school: every year young musicians aged 14–25 learn from international artists and finish with a performance on the festival stage.
