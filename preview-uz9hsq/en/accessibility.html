Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use the festival website and come to the shows.

Accessibility level

The site follows Israeli Standard IS 5568, based on the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA, and the Israeli accessibility regulations for services.

What the site does

  • Full keyboard navigation with a clear focus outline and a "skip to content" link.
  • Colour contrast that meets the standard for text and buttons.
  • A correct heading structure and text descriptions for images.
  • Tap targets sized for touch screens.
  • The device setting "reduce motion" is respected – animations are reduced.
  • The site is available in Hebrew and English and works on phones.

The accessibility menu

Every page has an accessibility tab on the side of the screen (the person icon). It opens a menu with these options:

  • Stop all motion and video – stops the background videos, the moving photo strips, the rotating spotlight and every animation.
  • Larger text – in two steps (A+ and A++), across the whole site.
  • High contrast – light (black text on white) or dark.
  • Black and white (monochrome) – for people with colour blindness.
  • Underline all links.
  • Strong keyboard focus – a thick frame around the element selected with the keyboard.

Your settings are saved in the browser and stay on as you move between pages and on later visits. "Reset all" returns the normal view. The menu itself works with the keyboard and screen readers, and is built into the site without an outside plugin.
