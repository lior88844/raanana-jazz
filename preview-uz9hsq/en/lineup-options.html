5 ways to show the line-up on the home page

Scroll down – each option with its own motion. Click a name to open the show.

12345
1

Moving names

Three rows, one per day, moving sideways on their own – every name the same size. Scrolling speeds them up; hovering stops the row and shows the artist photo.

2

Equal photo grid

The same tile for every show. Black and white, turning to colour on hover (on a phone – when it reaches the middle).

3

Day switcher

Three tabs – Thu, Fri, Sat. The day's shows as a timeline that slides when you switch days.

4

Rotating spotlight

One show at a time, in random order, changing every few seconds. Pauses on hover; arrows and dots to move.

5

Postcards

A postcard for every show, scattered like on a table, in a new order each visit. They land as you scroll.
